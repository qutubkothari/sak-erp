## Introduction

Welcome to **SAK HR Performance Management System** - your comprehensive solution for managing employee performance evaluations, goal tracking, 360-degree feedback, and development planning. This guide will walk you through everything you need to know to use the system effectively.

**No technical knowledge required!** This guide is written for end users who want to understand what the system does and how to use it.

---

## What is SAK HR?

SAK HR is a **web-based performance management application** designed specifically for organizations operating in the **UAE (United Arab Emirates)**. It helps companies:

- ✅ Conduct structured performance reviews
- ✅ Set and track employee goals
- ✅ Collect 360-degree feedback from peers and managers
- ✅ Generate official appraisal letters and reports
- ✅ Ensure compliance with UAE labor standards
- ✅ Make fair compensation and promotion decisions

### Why SAK HR?

Traditional performance reviews often involve:
- ❌ Scattered Excel spreadsheets
- ❌ Lost email feedback
- ❌ Inconsistent rating standards
- ❌ Manual report generation
- ❌ No audit trail

**SAK HR solves these problems** by providing a centralized, automated, and audit-ready performance management system.

## Key Features Overview

### 🎯 1. Goals & Objectives
**What it does:** Helps employees set SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound) goals aligned with company competencies.

**Example:** A sales manager might set a goal like "Increase team revenue by 15% by Q4 2026" linked to the "Business Acumen" competency.

---

### 📝 2. Self Assessment
**What it does:** Employees evaluate their own performance against competencies, KPIs, merits, and demerits before manager review.

**Example:** An employee rates themselves on "Communication Skills" (1-5 scale) and provides evidence of achievements.

---

### 👔 3. Manager Review
**What it does:** Managers review their direct reports, provide ratings (competencies, KPIs, merits, demerits), and make compensation recommendations.

**Example:** A manager reviews their team member's self-assessment, adjusts ratings if needed, and recommends a 10% salary increase.

---

### 🔄 4. Review Cycles
**What it does:** HR creates structured review periods (annual, semi-annual, quarterly) with defined start/end dates and calibration windows.

**Example:** "Annual Review 2026" running from Jan 1 - Dec 31, with calibration in November.

---

### 👥 5. Employees & Managers
**What it does:** Maintains organizational structure with reporting relationships and evaluation ownership.

**Example:** Shows that "Ahmed Ali" reports to "Sara Khan" (IT Manager) in the "Technology" department.

---

### 🎓 6. Evaluation Criteria (Competencies, KPIs, Merits & Demerits)
**What it does:** Defines the evaluation questionnaire (soft skills, performance metrics, merits, demerits) with weights, and lets HR add/remove items.

**Example:** 
- **Competency:** "Leadership" (weight: 20%)
- **KPI:** "Customer Satisfaction Score" (target: >4.5/5, weight: 15%)

---

### 📊 7. Rating Scales
**What it does:** Creates standardized score bands with labels for consistent evaluations.

**Example:**
- 4.5-5.0 = "Exceptional"
- 3.5-4.4 = "Exceeds Expectations"
- 2.5-3.4 = "Meets Expectations"
- Below 2.5 = "Needs Improvement"

---

### ⚖️ 8. Calibration
**What it does:** Allows managers and HR to align ratings across teams to ensure fairness before finalizing evaluations.

**Example:** Three managers meet to review all "Exceptional" ratings to ensure consistency.

---

### 💬 9. 360-Degree Feedback
**What it does:** Collects anonymous feedback from peers, subordinates, and cross-functional partners.

**Example:** A project manager requests feedback from 3 team members and 2 stakeholders about their collaboration skills.

---

### 📄 10. Appraisal Letters
**What it does:** Generates official, professional appraisal letters with ratings, feedback, and compensation details in PDF format.

**Example:** Creates a formal letter: "Dear Ahmed, Your performance rating for 2026 is 4.2/5.0 (Exceeds Expectations)..."

---

### 📈 11. Analytics
**What it does:** Provides real-time dashboards showing evaluation progress, rating distributions, and trends.

**Example:** Shows that 85% of evaluations are complete, average rating is 3.8/5.0, and 12% of employees are in "Needs Improvement."

---

### 📊 12. Reports
**What it does:** Generates comprehensive Excel exports and PDF reports for enterprise reporting.

**Example:** Exports all employee performance data to Excel for board presentations or government compliance.

---

### 🚀 13. Improvement Plans
**What it does:** Creates structured development plans for employees who need performance support.

**Example:** For an employee with low "Technical Skills" rating, create a 90-day plan with training courses and milestones.

---

### ✅ 14. Evaluations
**What it does:** Central hub to launch evaluations, track approval workflows, and finalize ratings.

**Example:** HR launches 50 evaluations, tracks progress (Draft → Self Review → Manager Review → HR Review → Finalized).

---

### 🔔 15. Notifications
**What it does:** Real-time alerts for pending actions, approvals, deadlines, and an in-app inbox for managing items.

---

### 🧾 16. Activity & Audit Trail
**What it does:** Logs evaluation activity (self submissions, manager reviews, approvals) for transparency and audit readiness.

**Example:** "Your self-assessment for Annual Review 2026 is due in 3 days."

### 📝 Self Assessment

#### When to Use It
- At the beginning of your performance review cycle
- After you receive a notification: "Time to complete your self-assessment"

#### How to Complete Self Assessment

**Step 1:** Click **"Self Assessment"** from the main dashboard

**Step 2:** You'll see your evaluation with sections:
- **Competencies:** Rate yourself on each competency (e.g., Communication, Leadership)
- **Merits:** Rate positive contributions (e.g., Innovation, Customer Appreciation)
- **Demerits:** Rate improvement areas (e.g., Policy Compliance, Quality Lapse)
- **KPIs:** Enter your achievement for each KPI (e.g., "Achieved 95% of sales target")

**Step 3:** For each competency:
1. Select your **rating** (1-5 scale or as defined by your company)
2. Write **comments** explaining your rating with examples:
   - Example: "I led 3 cross-functional projects this year, improving team collaboration by implementing weekly stand-ups."

**Step 4:** For each KPI:
1. Enter your **actual achievement** (e.g., "$500,000 revenue generated")
2. System will show your **target** and calculate **% achievement**
3. Add **comments** if needed

**Step 5:** (Optional) Add **evidence links** (documents, dashboards, reports)

**Step 6:** (Optional) Click **"Save Draft"** if you want to continue later

**Step 7:** Review your entries and click **"Submit Self Assessment"**

**Step 8:** Evaluation moves to "Manager Review" stage

#### What Happens Next
- Your manager receives a notification
- Manager reviews your self-assessment
- Manager provides their own ratings and comments
- You'll receive notification when manager review is complete

### ⚖️ Calibration

*(For Managers and HR)*

#### What is Calibration?
A meeting where managers and HR review evaluations together to ensure ratings are fair and consistent across the organization.

**Example Problem:** Manager A rates everyone 5/5, Manager B rates everyone 3/5 for similar performance. Calibration fixes this.

#### How Calibration Works

**Step 1:** HR creates a **Calibration Session**:
- **Session Name:** "2026 Annual Review Calibration"
- **Review Cycle:** Annual Review 2026
- **Date:** November 25, 2026
- **Participants:** All department managers + HR

**Step 2:** HR adds employees to calibration:
- Select employees with ratings to review
- Typically focus on:
  - All "Exceptional" ratings (to verify they're truly exceptional)
  - All "Needs Improvement" ratings (to ensure fairness)
  - Borderline cases (e.g., 3.4 vs 3.5)

**Step 3:** During calibration meeting:
1. Review each employee's rating
2. Discuss evidence and justification
3. Compare across teams
4. Adjust ratings if needed for consistency

**Step 4:** Make calibration decisions:
- **Keep Rating:** Rating is appropriate
- **Increase Rating:** Employee deserves higher rating
- **Decrease Rating:** Rating is too generous
- **Discussion Notes:** Record reasons for decision

**Step 5:** Click **"Finalize Calibration"**

**Step 6:** Adjusted ratings are updated in the system

#### Evaluation Workflow Stages

Each evaluation goes through these stages:

```
1. DRAFT → 2. SELF_REVIEW → 3. MANAGER_REVIEW → 
4. HR_REVIEW → 5. CALIBRATION → 6. FINALIZED
```

**Stage Details:**

| Stage | Who Acts | What Happens |
|-------|----------|--------------|
| **1. DRAFT** | HR | Evaluation created but not yet active |
| **2. SELF_REVIEW** | Employee | Employee completes self-assessment |
| **3. MANAGER_REVIEW** | Manager | Manager provides ratings and feedback |
| **4. HR_REVIEW** | HR | HR reviews for compliance and completeness |
| **5. CALIBRATION** | Managers + HR | Ratings aligned across organization |
| **6. FINALIZED** | HR | Evaluation locked, appraisal letter generated |

#### How to Track Evaluations

- Open an evaluation to view the **Activity** timeline

### Complete Annual Review Workflow

Here's how a typical annual performance review works from start to finish:

#### **Phase 1: Setup (HR) - December/January**

**Week 1-2:**
1. HR creates "Annual Review 2026" cycle
2. HR confirms competencies and KPIs are up to date
3. HR confirms rating scales are configured
4. HR confirms all employees have assigned managers

**Week 3:**
5. HR launches evaluations for all employees
6. System sends notifications to all employees

---

#### **Phase 2: Self-Assessment (Employees) - January/February**

**Weeks 4-8:**
1. Employee receives notification: "Complete self-assessment by Feb 15"
2. Employee logs in and goes to "Self Assessment"
3. Employee reviews competencies and KPIs
4. Employee rates themselves and provides evidence/examples
5. Employee reviews their goals progress
6. Employee submits self-assessment
7. Manager receives notification: "Self-assessment ready for review"

---

#### **Phase 3: Manager Review (Managers) - February/March**

**Weeks 9-12:**
1. Manager receives notification
2. Manager reviews employee's self-assessment
3. Manager provides their own ratings (can agree or differ)
4. Manager writes feedback (strengths, development areas)
5. Manager makes compensation recommendations
6. Manager meets with employee to discuss (optional but recommended)
7. Manager submits review
8. HR receives notification: "Manager review completed"

---

#### **Phase 4: HR Review (HR) - March**

**Weeks 13-14:**
1. HR reviews all completed evaluations
2. HR checks for:
   - Completeness (all fields filled)
   - Fairness (ratings align with evidence)
   - Policy compliance (compensation within budgets)
3. HR flags any issues for discussion
4. HR approves evaluations or sends back for revision

---

#### **Phase 5: Calibration (Managers + HR) - March/April**

**Weeks 15-16:**
1. HR creates calibration session
2. HR invites all managers and senior leadership
3. During calibration meeting:
   - Review all "Exceptional" ratings (verify they're justified)
   - Review all "Needs Improvement" ratings (ensure fairness)
   - Discuss borderline cases
   - Align ratings across teams for consistency
4. Make adjustments where needed
5. Finalize calibrated ratings

---

#### **Phase 6: Finalization & Communication (HR) - April**

**Weeks 17-18:**
1. HR finalizes all evaluations
2. HR generates appraisal letters for all employees
3. HR reviews compensation changes with Finance
4. HR schedules meetings with employees
5. Managers meet with employees to deliver results
6. HR distributes appraisal letters (PDF)
7. HR processes compensation changes in payroll system
8. Employees can view results in system

---

#### **Phase 7: Development Planning (Ongoing) - April onwards**

**Weeks 19+:**
1. Employees with "Needs Improvement" ratings:
   - HR creates Improvement Plans
   - Assigns mentors and resources
   - Sets milestones and check-ins

2. All employees:
   - Set new goals for next year
   - Create development plans based on feedback
   - Enroll in training courses

3. HR:
   - Archives completed review cycle
   - Generates reports for leadership
   - Plans next review cycle

## Frequently Asked Questions

### For Employees

**Q: When will I know my performance review is due?**  
A: You'll receive a notification 2-4 weeks before your self-assessment deadline. Check the notification bell icon regularly.

**Q: Can I save my self-assessment and come back later?**  
A: Yes! The system auto-saves as you go. You can log out and continue later. Just click "Submit" when you're ready.

**Q: What if I disagree with my manager's rating?**  
A: You can discuss your concerns with your manager during your review meeting. You can also escalate to HR if you feel the rating is unfair. Provide specific evidence to support your case.

**Q: Can I see my previous year's evaluations?**  
A: Yes, go to "Evaluations" and filter by past review cycles. You can view your history and track your progress over time.

**Q: Who can see my 360 feedback responses?**  
A: Feedback you provide is anonymous to the person being reviewed. Only aggregated results are shown (not individual responses). HR may see raw data for serious issues only.

**Q: How are my overall ratings calculated?**  
A: It's a weighted average:
- Example: Communication (20% weight) × 4.0 rating = 0.8
- Leadership (15% weight) × 3.5 rating = 0.525
- Sum all weighted scores = Overall rating

---

### For Managers

**Q: How many people should I include in calibration?**  
A: Typically focus on:
- All top 10% performers (to verify "Exceptional" ratings)
- All bottom 10% performers (to ensure fairness)
- Anyone on the border between rating levels

**Q: What if my team member hasn't completed their self-assessment?**  
A: Send a reminder through the system. If still not completed by deadline, you can proceed with manager review only, but self-assessment is highly recommended.

**Q: Can I change my ratings after submission?**  
A: Before finalization: Yes, you can edit. After HR review/calibration: You must request HR to unlock the evaluation. After finalization: No changes allowed (locked for audit purposes).

**Q: What's a fair distribution of ratings on my team?**  
A: Typical distribution (not a requirement):
- Exceptional: 10-15%
- Exceeds: 35-40%
- Meets: 40-45%
- Needs Improvement: 5-10%

Adjust based on actual performance, but if everyone is "Exceptional," be ready to justify in calibration.

---

### For HR Administrators

**Q: Can we customize competencies for different departments?**  
A: Yes, you can create department-specific competencies or role-specific competencies and assign them accordingly.

**Q: What if we need to extend the review cycle deadline?**  
A: Go to "Review Cycles," select the active cycle, and click "Edit Dates." Update deadlines and save. Employees will receive notifications of the change.

**Q: How do we handle employees who join mid-cycle?**  
A: You can launch individual evaluations for new employees at any time, or wait for the next review cycle. For probation reviews, create a "Probation Review" cycle type.

**Q: Can we export data to integrate with our payroll system?**  
A: Yes, use the Excel export feature to download all compensation recommendations, then import into your payroll system.

**Q: How long is evaluation data retained?**  
A: All evaluation data is retained indefinitely for audit and compliance purposes. You can archive old review cycles but data remains accessible.

## Tips & Best Practices

### For Employees

✅ **DO:**
- Complete self-assessments on time (don't wait until last minute)
- Provide specific examples in comments ("I led the Q3 project, delivering 2 weeks early")
- Be honest about areas for development
- Update your goals progress regularly
- Review your job description before self-assessment
- Save drafts frequently

❌ **DON'T:**
- Rate yourself all 5/5 without evidence
- Leave comments blank
- Copy-paste from previous year
- Ignore notifications
- Wait for manager to prompt you

---

### For Managers

✅ **DO:**
- Review self-assessments thoroughly before providing your ratings
- Meet with your team member to discuss (don't just submit remotely)
- Provide balanced feedback (strengths AND development areas)
- Give specific examples in comments
- Be prepared to justify your ratings in calibration
- Document performance issues throughout the year (not just during review)
- Align compensation recommendations with budget

❌ **DON'T:**
- Rate everyone the same to avoid difficult conversations
- Surprise employees with low ratings they haven't heard about before
- Rush through reviews
- Copy-paste comments between employees
- Recommend compensation changes you can't justify
- Skip calibration meetings

---

### For HR Administrators

✅ **DO:**
- Communicate review cycle dates early and often
- Send regular reminders as deadlines approach
- Provide training to managers on how to conduct reviews
- Monitor completion rates and follow up on stragglers
- Review data for anomalies (e.g., one manager rating everyone 5/5)
- Prepare calibration agenda in advance
- Archive completed cycles for audit trail

❌ **DON'T:**
- Launch review cycles without communicating to employees
- Set unrealistic deadlines
- Skip calibration (it's essential for fairness)
- Finalize evaluations with errors or missing data
- Forget to generate appraisal letters
- Delete historical data
